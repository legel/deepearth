# Scorecards — where the campaign actually stands

Every loop publishes two files in its `program/` directory:

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `scorecard.txt` | **the numbers.** Current best per metric, fair gain, diagnosis. Plain text, skimmable, committed to git, **generated by the loop's harness after every run — never hand-edited.** |
| `scorecard.md` | **the meaning.** What each metric is, how a record is earned, what is excluded and why. |

`records.json` is gitignored and single-owner, so `scorecard.txt` is the only way to read progress from
the repository. That is why it is generated rather than written: a hand-maintained scorecard drifts, and
a drifted scorecard is worse than none.

## How to read any of them

```
   record       the current best for that metric — a PROBE record: discovery, not science
   fair-gain    the metric MINUS the strongest FAIR baseline (trained generic PE / RFF / MLP)
   read         the diagnosis, and therefore which lever comes next:

                INPUT-LIMITED      does not beat a generic PE → DATA lever, change the channel
                ENCODER-LIMITED    contributes <25% of the score → ARCHITECTURE lever
                EARNING            contributes ≥25% of the score → push the mechanism further
                STALE-GAIN         the stored gain is a CHANNEL advantage, not encoder-vs-PE — re-measure
                NO-FAIR-BASELINE   no control was recorded, so the bottleneck is undiagnosable
                pre-contract       record predates the result contract; its metric name was never stored
```

**A record is not a result.** It becomes a claim only by clearing its loop's evidence standard: ≥5
matched seeds, block bootstrap, no registered regression, reproducible from a committed tree. Anything
short of that is discovery — which is most of what these files contain today, and they say so.

Three live caveats worth knowing before trusting a number:

- **The standing records are single-seed high draws, and at least one is measurably so.** Running
  `family_from_env` at 3 matched seeds gives 0.142318 / 0.122941 / 0.139142 — mean **0.1348**, sd
  **0.0085**. The board's record is 0.1423, which is *seed 0*: the best draw, sitting +0.0075 above the
  mean, inside one standard deviation. With the spread measured, the barrier to beat that record is
  2σ = 0.017 — so **the record could not clear its own bar.** Every record predating the noise barrier
  should be assumed to be the maximum of its reruns until re-measured at ≥5 seeds.

- `spacetime` `family_from_env` is stored with a channel gain (`+0.0411 ENV vs best-coord-PE`) while a
  live run reports `−0.0072 Earth4D vs RFF`. Flagged `STALE-GAIN`; the honest read is INPUT-LIMITED.
- The `spacetime` harness has no seed loop yet, so a single-seed noise delta can still become a record.
  It happened twice on 2026-07-30 and both were reverted, with reasons in the ledger.
