**DeepEarth** is a [_self-supervised_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-supervised_learning), [_multi-modal_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multimodal_learning), [_spatio-temporal_](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/social-sciences/spatio-temporal-model) AI world model for the planet.

#### News:
- _October 31, 2026_  
  **State-of-the-art climate simulation.** DeepEarth simulates sun, water, wind, temperature, and carbon across global flux towers more accurately than all existing models. See [_report_](https://deepearth.ai/fluxfill/#report), [_code_](https://github.com/legel/deepearth/tree/main/models/flux/fill).

- _October 5, 2026_  
  **#1 model of native plant habitats.** DeepEarth has exceeded all published models for predicting distributions of all native plant species across the United States. See [_report_](https://deepearth.ai/native-habitats/), [_code_](https://github.com/legel/deepearth/tree/main/models/habitat/plant)

- _July 16, 2026_  
  **NASA award.** NASA's Commercial Satellite Data Acquisition has awarded DeepEarth 5 million km² of remote sensing data, approximately $200,000 in value. See [_program_](https://science.nasa.gov/earth-science/csda/).

- _March 7, 2026_  
  **Paper on arXiv.** _"Self-Supervised Multi-Modal World Model with 4D Space-Time Embedding"_, following peer-review through the 2026 World Modeling Workshop is now on arXiv. See [_paper_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07039).

- _December 22, 2025_  
  **10x faster.** CUDA batch processing has increased by 10x. See [_commit_](https://github.com/legel/deepearth/commit/69f5be4e35c29df43c302bd3580b47d3911997e3). 

- _December 19, 2025_  
  **Supercomputing award.** US DOE National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center has awarded a DeepEarth team with supercomputing access in 2026. See [_program_](https://www.nersc.gov/).
    
- _November 16, 2025_  
  **23% error reduction in space-time encoder.** Learned hash probing in Earth4D led to state-of-the-art R² on an ecological forecasting benchmark. See [_commit_](https://github.com/legel/deepearth/commit/aa2a4b7).

- _October 27, 2025_  
  **Battle-hardened (_x_, _y_, _z_, _t_) AI.**  For our spatio-temporal multi-resolution hash encoding, we've fixed a numerical bug in NVIDIA's CUDA kernels based on profiling of hash collisions. See [_commit_](https://github.com/legel/deepearth/pull/7).

- _September 30, 2025_  
  **Presentation at top AI lab.** Thanks to the Allen Institute for AI for hosting a 1 hour talk with scientists pioneering AI foundation models for the planet. See [_video_](  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SHJwCInICiA).

- _August 8, 2025_  
  **NSF summer school program.** NSF funded a week-long "Spatial AI for Disaster Resilience" summer school program in Boulder, Colorado. 5 PhD students researched and developed DeepEarth. See [_program_](https://i-guide.io/summer-school/summer-school-2025/).

- _June 23, 2025_  
  **Workshop in Chicago.** NSF funded a 3 hour workshop on DeepEarth in Chicago for a "GeoAI for Sustainability" conference. 3 professors, 5 postdocs, and 2 PhD students contributed. See [_program_](https://i-guide.io/forum/forum-2025/workshops/).

#### Changing the World
[DeepEarth.ai](https://deepearth.ai) now simulates sun, wind, water, soil, & plants across the United States with state-of-the-art accuracy at centimeter scale.
