## Steering interpretation

This section is an interpretation for agents, not a quotation from Lance.

1. Treat PR #30's continuous absolute temporal field as a reasonable first step, not the completed causal space-time architecture.
2. Make the relative space-time encoder the principal follow-up: a bounded, LSTM-like causal window that learns invariant dynamics from past observations.
3. Institutionalize the applicable Entropy4D principles in `science.md`, including perceptive fields, mixed equivariant and non-equivariant bases, iterative focusing, persistent causal state, and prediction of future canonical representations.
4. Preserve compatibility with the absolute Earth4D encoder; Entropy4D principles may improve both encoders but primarily specify the relative path.
5. Plan for spatially situated patch-level aerial embeddings instead of collapsing every NAIP/DINOv3 observation to one CLS or mean vector. Those patches should retain their positions so a 4D GNN can model local structure and dynamics.
6. Treat the patch-token expansion as a major future data phase, expected to increase the dataset by more than 10x. Lance supports it when the overall system is ready; this feedback is not a request to expand the dataset prematurely.
7. Evaluate architectural claims causally: past-only inputs, persistent state, future representation prediction, arbitrary-view or coordinate robustness, and matched comparisons against the present Earth4D paths.

## Candidate steering sequence

1. Amend `science.md` with the relative-encoder and Entropy4D contract.
2. Define focused, causal benchmarks before implementation, including temporal holdouts and leak guards.
3. Prototype the smallest useful perceptive-field population over currently available observation tokens.
4. Validate persistent focusing/reconfiguration and future canonical-state prediction.
5. Design the patch-level NAIP/DINOv3 data representation and storage plan.
6. Activate patch-level inputs only after the core relative architecture and evaluation contract are stable.
