# The Daru (2024) pipeline, step by step: purpose, necessity, measured value

For each step: what it does, why it is there, what we measured, and whether it earns its place at 240 m.
Evidence comes from the pilot species (*Echinacea purpurea*, *Larrea tridentata*) and is updated as the
benchmark set (114 species with Daru rasters) completes. Details and dates: `scientific_provenance.md`.

| # | Step | Purpose | Necessity | Measured so far | Verdict at 240 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GBIF occurrences | Raw evidence of where the species lives | Essential | Larrea: 98,876 records; Echinacea: 16,641 | Keep |
| 2a | WCVP name reconciliation | One accepted name per plant; native areas come from the same checklist | Essential; errors silently corrupt native status | A naive first-row match followed *misapplied* names and flipped native status for 106 species (fixed) | Keep, status-ranked |
| 2b | CoordinateCleaner | Remove centroids, institutions (cultivated), sea points, duplicates | Essential: institution and centroid records are systematically wrong places | Larrea 98,876 → 92,262 valid (−6.7%); Echinacea 16,641 → 15,490 (−6.9%) | Keep |
| 2c | Native-area filter (WCVP L3) | Keep only records inside the native range | Essential for a *native* range; cultivated plants are planted far outside it | Echinacea 15,490 → 11,631 (−25%: planted garden records); Larrea −0.2% | Keep |
| 2d | Spatial thinning | Reduce sampling clustering | Useful | Larrea 92,113 → 24,390 at ~1 km | Keep |
| 3 | Alpha hull (+land, +family range) | Turn points into a range envelope | In Daru's design it defines the presences (step 6a); hull ∩ family range needed morphological closing at 240 m | Slivers along state borders found and fixed | Keep as envelope; see 6a |
| 4 | Phylogeographic dispersal (SBM) | Limit the model to areas the species could reach | Conceptually right, practically inert: rates are ~1–4 km/yr, under Daru's 18 km cell, and his published calibration areas are whole occupied ecoregions | Footprint IoU with Daru: whole occupied ecoregions 0.83–0.85 vs literal hull ∩ ecoregions 0.47 | Calibration = whole occupied ecoregions; dispersal recorded, map-neutral |
| 5 | Bias-weighted background (KDE) | Make the background reflect sampling effort, cancelling observer bias | Described but **not used in Daru's fits**: his code draws a uniform background (D9) | Daru's maps match a uniform background (Spearman 0.86–0.87 vs 0.75–0.79); with hull presences uniform is also better on AIM (0.906 vs 0.879); with real occurrences no difference (0.987 vs 0.986) | Uniform for the Daru replica; bias-weighted kept for occurrence-trained models. Never pair bias-weighted background with hull-lattice presences: panel median AUC 0.623 vs 0.713 with uniform (below chance for Festuca, Lupinus) |
| 6a | 500 regular points on the hull as presences | Defend against sampling bias at ~18 km by ignoring where records cluster | **Costly at 240 m**: the map inside the hull becomes a climate envelope | AIM AUC (Larrea): hull 0.914 vs real thinned occurrences 0.986 (0.983 for plots > 10 km from any record); Daru's map 0.939 | Replace (variant run for all species; ledger L8) |
| 6b | WorldClim ~1 km + VIF < 5 | Climate predictors without collinearity | Essential; VIF port identical to `usdm` | 7–9 of 20 kept | Keep; add fine predictors (ledger L3/L10) |
| 6c | MaxEnt, β by 5-fold CV, 5 replicates | Fit and regularize; replicates average out split noise | Keep (reference model); fixed seed for reproducibility | CV picks β = 2 for both pilots | Keep |
| 7 | Projection | Map suitability | GPU closed form, identical to maxent.jar (4e-15) | 5–7 s per species over CONUS | Keep (engine replaced) |
| 8 | Threshold (per-replicate ESS, majority vote) | Presence/absence map | The threshold rule matters a great deal | Larrea AIM TSS: majority vote 0.52 vs 5th-percentile 0.675 (hull model); 0.84 with occurrence presences | Store both rules. **Chosen on independent plots (2026-10-04, 28 AIM / 12 FIA species): P5 for the occurrence-trained product** (sensitivity 0.96, TSS 0.733 AIM / 0.795 FIA vs ESS vote 0.580 / 0.653 and Daru 0.443 / 0.783); ESS vote kept in mode `daru` |
| 9 | Evaluation (AUC, TSS, Boyce) | Report model quality | Keep, but read TSS with care: phyloregion takes the *median* TSS across thresholds (not the max), so Daru's 0.42 understates discrimination | Larrea (Daru-faithful): AUC 0.86, TSS-median 0.39, TSS-max 0.61, Boyce 0.84 vs Daru's medians 0.91 / 0.42 / 0.87 | Keep both TSS definitions; add independent plots |
| — | 240 m resolution itself | Fine-scale placement | With WorldClim ~1 km input, **no measurable information below ~1 km** | 29 species × 2 modes (BLM AIM, `resolution_study.py`): 240 m vs its own 0.96 km aggregate, median −0.0002 / −0.0004 AUC; vs 18 km +0.004 (occurrences) / −0.0005 (faithful). The 6–8-species panel gain (+0.003 to +0.037) does not generalize | Keep the 240 m grid (exact, NLCD-aligned, ready for fine predictors); effective resolution is ~1 km until predictors that vary at 240 m are added (ledger L10) |
