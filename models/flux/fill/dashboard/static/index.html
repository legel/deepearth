FluxFill

Filling eddy-covariance tower records with a model of everything measured at every tower.

Mira Shivani Sankar, Lance Legel, Trevor Keenan. Quantitative Ecosystem Dynamics lab, University of California, Berkeley. Sponsored by Ecological Intelligence, Inc.

FluxFill learns the joint field of what is measured at and around eddy-covariance towers: carbon dioxide, water vapour, sensible heat and methane fluxes, the tower's meteorology, reanalysis drivers, and Sentinel-2 imagery. It learns by masking parts of 180-day windows and reconstructing them, with a probability distribution for every value. Its first application is filling the gaps in tower records. Every modality can be absent, so the same model fills a missing flux, a missing temperature sensor or a missing satellite image.

What it addresses

Limit of published methodsFluxFill and FluxFill-Bench
One target variable per model, driven by sensors that fail in the same outagesAll variables filled jointly; every modality can be masked, including a full station outage
No proper scoring rule; uncertainty calibrated on the test set or on short gaps onlyCRPS as the primary score; calibration by gap length on sites and years never used for fitting
No multi-site benchmark of contiguous gaps of weeks to months scored on sumsBlocks of 1 to 180 days at every season, scored on daily, monthly and window sums from joint samples
Site-specific models that do not learn from other towersOne model trained on all towers, tested on 20% of sites held out with 50 km spatial blocking
Ecosystem state seen through coarse vegetation indicesEvery Sentinel-2 acquisition over 300 m around each tower, embedded by the Clay foundation model

FluxFill-Bench

A window is 180 days of one site at half-hourly resolution. Only measured values are ever scored. Test sites, held-out years and masks are fixed before any model is fitted. Specification: BENCHMARK.md.

Regimes

R1Gap inside a record at a test site: the window's unmasked observations are visible.
R2Held-out final year at a training site.
R3No tower data: every tower channel masked for the whole window; reanalysis and imagery remain.

Mask families

M1Distribution-matched gaps (Irvin et al. 2021), for continuity with published numbers.
M2One contiguous block of 1, 7, 30, 90 or 180 days, uniform over the window, on all tower channels.
M3A real joint outage of all tower channels transplanted from another site-year.
M4One modality removed for the whole window: tower meteorology, reanalysis or Sentinel-2.

Scores

CRPS from joint samples (primary); CRPS and bias of daily, monthly and window sums; coverage of the 50%, 80% and 95% intervals and PIT histograms by gap length; MAE, MSE, RMSE, bias, R² and the weighted absolute percentage error of the predictive mean for comparison with the literature; correlation of daily anomalies and interannual variability; tail error. Definitions and formulas: Results, Metrics. Exact settings of every method: Methods.
